# Autumn menu draft

Theme, orchard fruit and Belgian spice. Launched start of October.

- Apple and quince tart, brown butter, a little juniper
- Pear and chocolate entremet, thin caramel
- Spiced plum financier
- Speculoos choux, light cream
- Treacle tart, the family one, in a small format

Notes, keep the spice quiet. The fruit should lead.
