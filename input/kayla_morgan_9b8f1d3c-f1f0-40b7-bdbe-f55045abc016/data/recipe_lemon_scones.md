# Lemon Scones (Kayla's) - makes 8
- 2 cups flour, 1/3 cup sugar, 1 Tbsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp salt
- zest of 2 lemons
- 6 Tbsp cold butter, cubed
- 1/2 cup cream, 1 egg, splash of vanilla

Cut butter into dry; add zest. Stir in wet just until it comes together. Pat to 1 inch, cut wedges, brush with cream, bake 400 F for 16-18 minutes. Glaze with lemon juice and powdered sugar.
