## Standing habits
- I never let materials go on order without seeing it first, even from yards I trust.
- I confirm install dates with the client by phone, then it goes in the calendar. The calendar is
  what I trust on the day.
- I keep the signed estimate as the contract. Drafts and concepts are just talk until I sign.
