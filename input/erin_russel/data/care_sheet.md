# Erin's Orchid Care Sheet — Show Prep (May 2026)

**Scope:** All six entries for the Southeast Regional Orchid Show, June 13–14.
**Author:** Erin Russell (greenhouse log, transcribed from greenhouse notebook).

---

## Environmental Rules (hard limits — do not exceed)

- **Root-zone / soil temperature must stay under 22°C at all times.** Anything above 22°C in the soil column risks root rot in Paphiopedilum and stress in Cattleya. Air temperature is allowed to run warmer but the root zone is monitored separately via the probe sensor.
- **Humidity target:** 50–65% in the greenhouse. Outside this band, run the misters or vent.
- **Air temperature:** 18–26°C daytime, 15–20°C nighttime. Air temperature is a soft target — the hard limit is the 22°C root-zone ceiling above.

## Watering Rules

- **Do not soak roots in standing water.** No pan-soaking, no bottom-watering trays, no leaving plants sitting in saucers of water. Roots must drain freely.
- Water from above, let drain fully, then return to bench.
- Phalaenopsis: water once weekly, more in summer.
- Paphiopedilum: water twice weekly, never let media fully dry.
- Cattleya: water when bark surface dries, approx. every 5–7 days.
- **Pan-soaking is explicitly prohibited** — this is the single most common mistake judges flag at shows. Standing water at the root collar invites crown rot within 48 hours.

## Repotting Triggers

- Roots wrapping the inside of the pot more than 1.5 turns.
- Bark medium broken down to small fragments or smelling sour.
- Yellowing pseudobulb bases on sympodial orchids.

## Show-Prep Reminders

- No fertilizer in the 14 days before judging — leaches into label-visible runoff.
- Stake bloom spikes at least 7 days before transport.
- Photograph each plant the morning of transport day for the prep packet.

*If a sensor or care log contradicts these rules, the rule wins. Flag the inconsistency in the prep packet for the orchid society record.*
